# TownTales book rules: what makes a great town history book

These rules apply to every TownTales book (towns and RMs), whoever writes it: Claude, the AI that drafts
revisions, or a person. A draft that breaks a rule gets fixed before it's printed.

## 1. Truth comes first
1. **Every fact has a source.** Each paragraph ends with its source, and every source is listed at the back.
2. **Never invent.** No made-up names, dates, numbers, quotes, feelings or causes. If we don't know, say less.
3. **Memories are labelled as memories.** "As remembered by Ann Kowalski" or "according to family memory".
   Records (newspapers, land grants, censuses, council minutes) can be stated as fact.
4. **When sources disagree, give both.** "The post office opened in 1883 (Henderson's Directory) or 1884 (the 1955 jubilee book)."
5. **Hedge the shaky things.** Use "about", "reportedly", "by 1910" rather than a precise date we can't back up.
6. **AI-read text is checked.** Anything an AI read off a photo or page is checked against the picture before it goes in.

## 2. Respect people
7. **Living people:** history only. No home addresses, phone numbers, health, money troubles, divorces or criminal matters.
   A living person appears only for public roles (mayor, team captain, business owner) or with permission.
8. **No gossip, no scandal-hunting.** If a hard event matters to the town (a fire, a crash, a murder trial), tell it plainly and briefly, from the record, without naming private victims' families.
9. **Everyone who built the town is in the book:** First Nations and Métis history of the land before and after settlement,
   every settler group (British, Scandinavian, Jewish, Ukrainian, German, French, Chinese café owners …), women by their full names
   (with maiden names where known), farm families as well as townsfolk.
10. **Fair words.** Today's respectful terms in our own writing. Old terms only inside a quotation, with the source.
11. **Names are spelled the way the family spelled them**, checked against the Roll of Remembrance and records. Full name on first mention.

## 3. Write so it's a pleasure to read
12. **Plain and warm.** Short sentences, everyday words, about a grade 8 reading level. The reader is often 70+.
13. **Canadian spelling** (colour, centre, neighbour, travelled).
14. **Show, with details.** A real name, a price, a date, a street beats "the community thrived".
15. **No filler or clichés:** never "nestled", "rich tapestry", "stood the test of time", "hustle and bustle", "a testament to", "bustling".
16. **Past tense for history; present tense only for what's still there.**
17. **One topic per paragraph, one era per chapter.** Dates in order inside each section.

## 4. The shape of the book (same skeleton for every town)
18. Cover: the town's **main street photo** (tell Travis when a town has none) and the title "<Town>: <short subtitle>".
19. Front: title page (with **revision number**), contents, a one-page welcome.
20. Chapters, in this order, skipping any with nothing real to say:
    1. The land and its first peoples (before the railway; treaties)
    2. Arrival: railway, surveys, homesteads, the first families
    3. Building the town: stores, elevators, hotels, post office, bank, newspaper
    4. Faith and learning: churches, schools, country school districts
    5. Hard years: fires, droughts, the Depression, the wars (and who served)
    6. Town life: sports, rinks, bands, fairs, clubs, celebrations
    7. Changing times: rail line and elevator closures, consolidation, the people who stayed
    8. The town today, briefly, as history (no current politics)
21. Back: **Roll of Remembrance** (names from TownTales' cemetery records), Town at a glance (timeline), Researching your family,
    Sources and picture credits, About TownTales, About this edition (what changed in this revision), Family notes pages.
22. RM books follow the same skeleton, with a chapter per town or hamlet and one on the country schools.

## 5. Pictures
23. **Only pictures we may print:** public domain (Canadian photos taken before 1949), openly licensed, or with written permission from the owner.
24. **Every picture has a caption:** what, where, when (or "about 1910"), and the credit.
25. **At least one picture per chapter.** If we don't have one, use a map or a document, never an unrelated stock photo.
26. Print quality: 300 DPI at printed size, greyscale for black-and-white interiors. No photos of living people without permission.

## 6. Copyright and other people's books
27. **Never copy an old local history book or a newspaper article word for word.** Tell the facts in our own words and credit it.
28. Short quotations (a sentence or two) are fine when the exact words matter, with the source.
29. A whole-book upload is a **source**, not text to reprint, unless the owner or committee has given written permission.

## 7. Printing (Lulu / KDP)
30. Trim 8.5×11 in (hardcover 8.25×11 in on KDP). Body text 11–12 pt, never under 10 pt. Margins at least 0.75 in, more on the spine side.
31. Even page count, at least 24 pages; fonts embedded; chapters start on a right-hand page; page numbers on every page but the cover.
32. The cover price covers Lulu's cost by page count, free shipping in Canada and at least 30% left over (see the pricing rules).

## 8. Revisions
33. **The revision number goes up every time the book changes**, and "About this edition" lists what's new.
34. **The AI only drafts.** Its draft waits in #admin until Travis approves it; nothing goes into a book automatically.
35. Before each revision is printed, re-run the checklist below.

## Checklist before a book is printed
- [ ] Every paragraph ends with a source; the sources page lists them all.
- [ ] No invented or unsourced names, dates or numbers; memories labelled.
- [ ] Nothing private about living people.
- [ ] First peoples, every settler group and the women of the town are in it.
- [ ] Main street on the cover; every picture captioned and allowed.
- [ ] Spell-check (Canadian); no clichés from rule 15.
- [ ] Roll of Remembrance up to date; revision number and "About this edition" updated.
- [ ] Print checks: page size, even pages, fonts embedded, 300 DPI pictures.
