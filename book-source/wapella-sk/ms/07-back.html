Sources and Picture Credits

Every fact in this book comes from one of the sources below. They are listed by the part of the book that relies on them most. Readers who want to go further should start with Cyril Leonoff’s article, the Dictionary of Canadian Biography entry on Abraham Klenman, and the Saskatchewan Historic Newspapers collection.

About TownTales

TownTales (towntales.ca) is a free online archive of small-town history across Canada. Anyone can add a photograph, a newspaper clipping, a page from an anniversary book or a memory of their own, and every town’s timeline is rebuilt from what people share, with the source of every fact shown.

This book was built from Wapella’s TownTales collection and from the published sources listed on the previous pages. It is printed when it is ordered, so each new edition includes what has been shared since.

Add to the next edition. Visit towntales.ca/wapella-sk and choose Share history. Photographs, letters, clippings and family stories are all welcome. Nothing goes public until it has been checked.

Corrections. If you find a mistake in this book, tell us at towntales.ca/contact. Corrections go into the next printing.

Remember someone. Every person in the Roll of Remembrance has a permanent page at towntales.ca, where families can add photographs and a life story.
